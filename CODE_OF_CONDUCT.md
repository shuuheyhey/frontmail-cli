# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Front CLI contributors, maintainers, and users must help keep project spaces
welcoming, respectful, and safe. Constructive disagreement is welcome;
harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, and the
publication of another person's private information are not.

Examples of positive participation include:

- giving specific, actionable, and respectful feedback;
- assuming good intent while addressing harmful impact;
- protecting Front tokens, customer data, and personal information; and
- accepting reasonable moderation decisions.

## Scope

This code applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
and other project spaces, as well as when someone publicly represents the
project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, commits, code, issues,
and other material that violates this code. They may warn, temporarily limit,
or permanently ban participants when necessary to protect the community.

Do not post sensitive conduct reports publicly. Contact a repository
maintainer through a private channel shown on their GitHub profile. Reports
will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical. Maintainers who
have a conflict of interest must not handle the report.
